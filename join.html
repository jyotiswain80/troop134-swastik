Start your Scouting adventure.

The easiest way to learn about the troop is to visit a Tuesday meeting.

Who can join?

Scouts BSA youth

Youth ages 11–17 can join, or age 10 with Arrow of Light or fifth grade completed.

First visit

Come as you are

Meet the Scouts and adult leaders, see how a meeting works, and ask questions about the program.

Meeting

Every Tuesday

7:00 PM at Journey Church, 450 Blue Ravine Road, Folsom, CA 95630.

Steps to join

  1. Visit a Tuesday meeting.
  2. Talk with the troop and learn about the program.
  3. Complete the current youth registration process.
  4. Stay connected with the troop as you begin your Scouting journey.
