Youth leadership

Scouts lead the troop.

Youth leadership positions give Scouts opportunities to plan, communicate, teach, and serve.

Senior Patrol Leader

Leads the troop's youth leadership team and helps guide meetings and the annual program.

Assistant Senior Patrol Leader

Supports the SPL and helps coordinate troop responsibilities.

Patrol Leader

Leads a patrol, communicates plans, and helps patrol members participate.

Scribe

Helps keep troop records and meeting information organized.

Quartermaster

Helps care for, organize, and prepare troop equipment.

Other positions

Troop roles can also include instructors, librarians, historians, guides, and other service positions.
