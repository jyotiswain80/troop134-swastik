About Troop 134

Scout-led, service-minded, adventure-ready.

Troop 134 is a Scouts BSA troop in Folsom, California, with 34 years of Scouting history.

Who we are

Troop 134 is a Scouts BSA troop for youth ages 11 to 17, or age 10 with Arrow of Light or fifth grade completed. We are chartered by American Legion Post 383 and are part of the Greater California Council, American River District.

How a Scout-led troop works

Scouts take meaningful responsibility for the troop. Patrols give Scouts a small team to lead and learn with. The Patrol Leaders' Council (PLC) helps plan the troop's program. A Scoutmaster conference gives Scouts a chance to reflect, set goals, and discuss progress with an adult leader.

What we do in a year

Our program can include weekly meetings, monthly campouts, summer camp, high-adventure opportunities, service projects, Courts of Honor, fundraising activities, and special troop traditions.

Scout Oath

"On my honor I will do my best To do my duty to God and my country to obey the Scout Law; to help other people at all times; To keep myself physically strong, mentally awake, and morally straight.''
